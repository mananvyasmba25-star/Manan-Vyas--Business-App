ABOUT THE BUSINESS

Road freight planning, made clearer.

LoadMatch is designed to make choosing a freight option and understanding the likely cost simpler for businesses moving goods by road.

LM LoadMatchFREIGHT PLANNING & ESTIMATION
↗

What the company does

LoadMatch helps shippers explore LTL/shared-load and FTL/full-truck transport options, review an indicative road-distance and price estimate, and submit an enquiry to discuss a shipment. The goal is to make freight planning easier before a final transporter quote is confirmed.

◎

Target customers

Small and medium businesses, manufacturers, wholesalers, distributors, retailers, e-commerce sellers, traders, freight coordinators, and individuals who need to move commercial goods without spending time comparing every transport option manually.

⌖

Service area

The initial focus is Gujarat and Rajasthan, with routes around business centres such as Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot, Jaipur, Jodhpur, and Udaipur. Actual serviceability depends on PIN-code validation and transport-partner coverage.

₹

Key value proposition

One place to enter pickup and delivery details, compare shared-load versus full-truck options, and see a transparent indicative cost breakdown. The concept is designed to reduce uncertainty in freight planning and support better use of available vehicle capacity.

Prototype note: price estimation is interactive, but live vehicle inventory, confirmed bookings, and dispatch operations require integration with transport partners and a backend system.
